# 05 - Research

Reference material, background reading, and notes your agents accumulate.

## Purpose

Agents read, search, and synthesize information during their work.
The useful stuff should land here so it doesn't disappear into a chat log.

This folder is a shared knowledge base. One agent's research is available
to every other agent in future sessions. Over time it becomes a real asset.

## What belongs here

- Summaries of articles, reports, or documentation
- Competitive or market research
- Technical reference notes (how does X work, what are the tradeoffs)
- Background on a topic your swarm revisits repeatedly
- Anything an agent found useful that a future agent might need

## What doesn't belong here

- Active project work (that goes in `02 - Projects/`)
- Task output (that lives in the task file or project folder)

## Suggested structure

Organize by topic or domain, not by date. You want to find things
by subject, not by when you researched them.

```
05 - Research/
├── industry-topic-1/
│   ├── overview.md
│   └── competitor-analysis.md
└── technical-topic/
    └── how-it-works.md
```

## Tips

- Lead every research note with a one-sentence summary
- Note the source and date at the top — research goes stale
- Link related notes to each other
