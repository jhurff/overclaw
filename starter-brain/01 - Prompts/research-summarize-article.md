# Prompt: Summarize Article

## Instructions for the agent

Read the content below and produce a structured summary.

**Format:**
- **One-sentence takeaway** — the single most important point
- **Key points** — 3 to 5 bullet points, each one concrete and specific
- **Why it matters** — one short paragraph on relevance to our work
- **Open questions** — anything this raises that we should explore further

Keep the summary tight. No filler. If something isn't worth noting, leave it out.
